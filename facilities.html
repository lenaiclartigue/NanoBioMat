Inside the lab

nanoScale Biomagnetics

Magnetic hyperthermia — from suspensions to small animals

The setup comprises three modules: an adiabatic module for suspensions, a module for cellular studies and a small-animal module. The photo shows the adiabatic module for suspensions.

Flash nanoprecipitation

Two streams are injected simultaneously into a PTFE confined impinging jets (CIJ) mixer. Rapid mixing allows nanoassemblies to form, which are collected in an aqueous bath.

In the videos, the yellow phase contains curcumin dissolved in THF; the orange phase also contains iron oxide nanoparticles dispersed in THF. In both cases, the clear phase is an aqueous polymer solution.

Flash nanoprecipitation: Johnson & Prud’homme, 2003 (new tab). CIJ setup adapted from Han et al., 2012 (new tab).

Reference publication

  • B. K. Johnson, R. K. Prud’homme, Aust. J. Chem. 2003, 56, 1021–1024. doi: 10.1071/CH03115.
  • J. Han et al., J. Pharm. Sci. 2012, 101, 4018–4023. doi: 10.1002/jps.23259.

Cell culture

A cell culture space supports the preparation and maintenance of cultures for studying interactions between nanoassemblies and cells. It includes a microbiological safety cabinet, a CO₂ incubator and an ALLSHENG AMR-100 microplate reader for absorbance measurements in 96-well plates.
