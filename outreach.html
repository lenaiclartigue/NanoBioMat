Science outreach

Sharing nanomedicine through observation, experimentation and discussion.

Science for everyone

Exploring the world of nanoparticles

How can we introduce nanomedicine to children? Everyday observations and simple experiments provide a starting point for discussing mixtures, very small objects and their use in medicine.

These outreach activities complement our university teaching and connect research in chemistry and nanomedicine with questions from younger audiences.

School workshop

Nano gets ready in the laboratory

An introduction to nanomedicine through mixtures and emulsions.

Audience: Primary school children, particularly the French cycle 2 age group, and their teachers.

From visible mixtures to the nanoscale

The workshop uses coloured liquids and soap to compare mixtures and discuss emulsions. It provides a concrete starting point for explaining how materials can be dispersed as small droplets and for introducing the preparation of nanomedicines.

01 / Observe

Compare mixtures

Describe what happens when liquids are brought together.

02 / Experiment

Explore emulsions

Discuss the role of soap and compare the appearance of the mixtures.

03 / Connect

Discover nanomedicine

Use these observations to introduce small-scale formulations and their role in medicine.

The visible mixtures are teaching models: they do not by themselves demonstrate that nanoparticles have been produced.
